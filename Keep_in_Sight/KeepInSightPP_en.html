Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application “Keep in Sight” handles user information.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data on external servers.

Notes may contain photos, text, tags, expiration dates, and reminder settings. This information is stored locally on the user’s device only and is not accessible to the developer or third parties.

Photos and Camera

Camera access is used only when the user chooses to take a photo for a note. Photo Library access is used only when the user chooses to add existing photos.

Photos added to notes and generated previews are stored locally as part of the app’s data and are not uploaded by the app to external servers.

Notifications

If the user enables reminders, the app uses local notifications to remind the user before a note’s expiration date. Reminder data is processed locally on the device.

Data Storage

Notes, photos, previews, tags, and app settings are stored locally on the device.

The app does not provide cloud storage or synchronization for user notes.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

Security

User data is stored locally on the device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and backups.

Data Deletion

Users can delete individual notes and archived notes within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data, subject to the device operating system and any device backups controlled by the user.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
