Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application “Got It All” handles user information.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers.

Users may create and save shopping lists, add items with quantities and units, and mark items as purchased. All this information is stored locally on the user’s device using CoreData and is not accessible to the developer or any third parties.

Data Usage

The app uses the locally stored data solely to display and manage the user’s shopping lists within the application.

No data is ever transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or any third‑party endpoints.

Data Storage

All user data (lists, items, quantities, completion status, and pinned items) is stored exclusively on the device.

The app does not upload, back up, or process this data on any external servers.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use:

  • analytics services
  • advertising networks
  • tracking tools

Security

Because all data remains on the user’s device, the security of that data depends on the device’s own protection mechanisms (passcode, biometrics, etc.). Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
